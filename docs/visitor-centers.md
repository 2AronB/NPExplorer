# Suggested visitor-center starting points

The planner retains 64 national-park records. Each has one editorial starting suggestion and, where useful, selected alternatives for another district, entrance or transport approach. These are practical orientation choices, not measured rankings of building size or popularity. They do not replace park routing points or change schedules, transport gateways, saved inputs or visit lengths.

Coordinates and facility names were reviewed November 4, 2026 against the NPS structured facility records referenced by NPS visitor pages, such as [Yellowstone's linked data](https://www.nps.gov/yell/yell_linked_data.json). Sequoia and Kings Canyon share [SEKI facility data](https://www.nps.gov/seki/seki_linked_data.json); they retain distinct suggested centers. Each published record links to its official coordinate source and visitor-information page. All 83 distinct visitor-information links for the 64 primary suggestions and 44 selected alternatives returned HTTP 200 during the review. Six stale upstream facility URLs were replaced with working official pages.

One default is deliberately not a claim that every trip should visit that center. For example, Island in the Sky and The Needles need different Canyonlands approaches; east and west Pinnacles have no connecting road. Outside-park orientation centers are labeled, and island or remote-air destinations are not presented as vehicle approaches. The visitor-center field is separate from the park's retained boundary/gateway coordinate.

The current [Hawaiʻi Volcanoes Welcome Center](https://www.nps.gov/havo/planyourvisit/welcome-center.htm) is used during the older Kīlauea center's renovation. Yosemite uses the [Valley Welcome Center](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/hours.htm). This is dated research, not a promise that the same facility operates in a future travel year. Opening hours, temporary closures, parking entrances, vehicle suitability, boat/air schedules and actual navigation were not exhaustively verified.

Pins use Google's documented [Maps URL search format](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started). They require no API key, use coordinates rather than a similarly named establishment, and do not automatically request driving directions or pass the visitor's saved home location to Google. A facility pin may identify a building rather than legal parking. Review official directions before using it as a navigation destination.

`data/park-visitor-centers.json` is the reviewed source. `node scripts/build-visitor-centers.mjs` validates coverage and regenerates the primary-only CSV. Tests verify inventory coverage, coordinate bounds, official source URLs, remote/district distinctions, pin URL encoding and the CSV. Raw downloaded source pages and private research notes are excluded from both repository publication and the site artifact.
