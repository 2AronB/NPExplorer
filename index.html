Driving estimate assumptions

Draft hours = estimated miles ÷ this speed + 15 minutes per leg. This is driving time, excluding breaks. It does not model actual road speeds.

Driving-day limits apply to driving hours. The stop allowance is shown separately as additional elapsed time and is included in the daily activity budget.
